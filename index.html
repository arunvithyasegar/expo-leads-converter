🛡️ Anna Incubator Privacy Notice & Data Policy

Event Data Protection Guidelines

Anna Incubator is committed to protecting the privacy of attendees, researchers, and partners at the 29th Energy Technology & Innovation Meet (ETM 2026), Goa.

  • Purpose of Collection: Contact details, interests, and notes are collected solely to evaluate incubation opportunities, facilitate industry-academia pilots, and conduct agreed follow-ups.
  • Consent Requirement: Explicit consent (Yes) is mandatory before initiating outbound calls, emails, or marketing. Outbound outreach is prohibited when consent is "No" or "Not asked".
  • Sensitive Data: Staff must not collect sensitive personal data (e.g. passwords, financial credentials, personal identification numbers) in conversation notes.
  • Data Retention: Leads are retained for 180 days post-ETM 2026 for incubation follow-up, after which inactive unconsented records are purged.
  • Data Subject Rights: Attendees may request access, rectification, or permanent erasure of their records at any time by contacting privacy@anna-incubator.org.
